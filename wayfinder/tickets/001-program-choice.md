## Question

Which established, evidence-backed or widely validated push-up program best fits going from a 50-rep to a 100-rep unbroken chest-to-floor max, training 3–4 structured sessions a week with no other training load? Compare at least: hundredpushups-style progressions, max-test percentage programs (e.g. Armstrong-style), grease-the-groove, and a hybrid of these. For the recommended one, extract its concrete rules: session structure (sets, reps, rest), weekly progression, when to repeat a week, deload, Max Test cadence, and what to do on a failed session. Also note any injury-risk guidance relevant to high-volume push-ups.

## Resolution

Hundred Pushups (hundredpushups.com), starting at Week 6, Column 1 (46–50 max). 3 Sessions a week on non-consecutive days, with a weekly Max Test in the 4th slot. The latest Max Test picks the column (46–50 C1, 51–60 C2, >60 C3). Repeat Week 6 until 100. No grease-the-groove by default. Several rules (failed session, above 60, Pain Flag threshold, deload triggers) are the researcher's proposals, not the program's; they are decided in the adjustment-rules ticket.
